DAY 1
01
PYQ
Share a previous year question paper
Open your phone gallery or Google Drive. Find any PYQ you saved before an exam. Upload it — PDF, photo, screenshot, anything works. Write 2 lines: which subject, which sem, and one honest line about whether it helped you or not.
What it can look like
"3rd sem Business Law PYQ from 2023. I used this the night before the exam. Unit 3 repeated exactly. Sharing so someone else doesn't have to hunt for it."
"Final year Marketing Management question paper. Got this from a senior. No idea if pattern changed but at least gives an idea of what to expect."
02
Notes
Share a set of notes — yours or someone else's
Handwritten, typed, PDF, photo of notebook — all fine. Pick any subject you have notes for. If your own notes are a mess, ask a friend and share theirs. Mention who made them and for which subject.
What it can look like
"My 2nd sem Accounts notes. Not pretty but covered everything that came in the exam. Take it."
"These are my friend Reena's OB notes. Honestly better than mine. Sharing with her permission."
DAY 2
03
PYQ
Second PYQ — different subject or sem
Same idea as Post 1. Different subject. If you noticed a pattern — which units repeat, which questions come every year — add that. Even 1 line of that makes the post 10x more useful than just uploading a file.
What it can look like
"HRM PYQ — 2022 and 2023 both. I noticed question 5 is almost always about performance appraisal. Make of that what you will."
"DBMS past papers 3rd sem. Questions from Unit 2 show up every single time without fail."
04
Event
An event that happened, is coming, or that you wish would happen
Three directions: something that already happened — describe it honestly. Something coming up that others might not know about. Or something you wish your college would organise but never does. All three are valid. Keep it specific to your college or area.
What it can look like
"We had a startup pitch event last week. Only 8 teams. The winner got a certificate. Felt underwhelming but the ideas were solid."
"I really want someone to organise a mock interview workshop for final years here. Placements are 3 months away and nobody has done anything."
DAY 3
05
Notes
Third set of notes — pick your most useful one
The one you actually studied from before your best exam result, or the one a senior gave you that saved you. If you have nothing left, ask 2 people in your class right now. Upload and credit them.
What it can look like
"Got these from my senior Arjun who graduated last year. He scored 88 in this. Uploading exactly as he gave it to me."
"Typed notes for Engineering Maths 1. Made these during revision week. Definitions and formulas only, no full theory."
06
Professor / Classroom / Common
Something everyone in your college silently relates to
Not naming anyone specifically. Just describing something everyone in your college knows — a professor's style, classroom situation, college WhatsApp group drama, attendance rules, canteen. The more specific to your college, the better. Generic posts get ignored.
What it can look like
"Our class WhatsApp group has 87 members. Only 4 people actually answer when someone asks a question. The other 83 watch silently."
"The college canteen closes at 4pm. The last class ends at 5pm. Nobody has ever explained this logic."
"Attendance marked present if you enter in the first 5 minutes. Half the class has perfected the art of walking in, getting marked, and disappearing."
DAY 4
07
PYQ
Third PYQ — add your own note about what to focus on
Last PYQ post. This time add a slightly more useful note — what unit to focus on, what type of questions repeat, what the examiner seems to like. Even if you're guessing based on pattern, say it. That's more valuable than just the file.
What it can look like
"Cost Accounting PYQ. From what I saw, problems from standard costing and marginal costing show up in every paper. Theory questions are usually from Unit 1."
"Entrepreneurship Development PYQ. Questions are almost the same every year with different wording."
08
Honest College Opinion
One honest thing about your college — good or bad, your call
Not a review, not a complaint. Just one real observation you would tell a friend who is deciding whether to join. It can be something good that nobody outside knows, or something frustrating that everyone inside knows. End with a question if you want replies.
What it can look like
"Placements at our college are decent for CS and ECE. For other branches you are mostly on your own. Nobody tells you this during admissions."
"Infrastructure is fine. Faculty is okay. The problem is zero industry connection. Every semester ends and nobody from outside has ever come to talk to us."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY
DAY 5
09
Notes
Formula sheet, summary, or cheatsheet — not full notes
Something more focused — a one-page formula sheet, important definitions only, a revision list. Something a student could use in the last hour before an exam. If you don't have one, make a simple one right now. It takes 10 minutes and will be the most useful thing you post.
What it can look like
"All important formulas for Engineering Maths 2 on one page. Made this the morning of my exam. Sharing it now."
"Key definitions for Business Environment — 20 terms that show up most often. Screenshot this."
10
College Story / Stress / Frustration
Something that happened to you in college that others will relate to
A real moment — funny, frustrating, awkward, stressful. It doesn't need a lesson or a conclusion. Just tell it like you would tell a friend over lunch. The more specific the better. Vague stories don't land. Specific ones do.
What it can look like
"Submitted an assignment at 11:58pm for an 11:59pm deadline. Portal crashed at 11:57. Still not over it."
"Our group project had 5 members. 2 did everything. 2 showed up on submission day. 1 we haven't seen since week 3. We all got the same grade."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY
DAY 6
11
Hostel / PG Life
Anything real about living away from home for college
Hostel rules, PG food, homesickness, managing money, finding a decent PG, first time cooking, anything. If you stay at home and commute, write about that — the travel, the exhaustion, missing things because of distance. Everyone will have felt some version of it.
What it can look like
"First month in PG I ate maggi 14 days straight. Not because I wanted to. Because I had no idea how to manage money and food at the same time."
"Hostel curfew is 9pm. College events mostly end at 9:30pm. Nobody has solved this conflict in 3 years."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY
12
Question for Peers / Juniors / Seniors
Ask something you actually want to know — from anyone in the hub
One real question directed at someone — juniors, seniors, students from other colleges, your own batch. Not a quiz question. Something you genuinely want answered. One question only. Short. Direct.
What it can look like
"To final year students — did your college actually help with placements or was it mostly your own effort? Asking for real."
"Do students from KLE or SDM have access to better industry connections than us? Genuinely curious how different the experience is."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY
DAY 7
13
Event
Second event post — different direction from post 4
If post 4 was a recap, make this one about something coming up or something you wish existed. If post 4 was a wishlist, make this a real recap. Mix it up. Mention other colleges if relevant — that's the whole point of C2 Club.
What it can look like
"Annual fest at our college is next month. Theme still not announced. Anyone from other colleges who came last year, was it worth it?"
"We had zero technical events this entire sem. Not one workshop, not one hackathon. Other colleges seem to have something every month."
14
Honest College Opinion
Second honest take — go one level deeper
Post 8 was one observation. This time something you have thought about more than once. Something that actually bothers you or genuinely impresses you about the system, the culture, or the people in your college.
What it can look like
"The culture in our college is to keep quiet and not ask questions. I spent 2 years thinking that was normal. Then I talked to students from other colleges and realised it's not."
"We have smart people here. The problem is there is no environment that pushes them. Everyone is just surviving sem by sem."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY
DAY 8
15
Future Plans
What you are planning to do after college — honest version
Not an inspiring speech. Just what you are actually thinking. MBA, job, competitive exam, startup, no idea yet — all valid. The confusion is as useful to share as the clarity. Others are thinking the same thing and nobody is saying it out loud.
What it can look like
"Planning to give CAT this year. Not because I am sure about MBA. Just because I don't know what else to do and this at least gives me 2 more years to figure it out."
"Genuinely no idea what comes next. Final year and still figuring it out. If anyone has been in this situation and found a direction, would love to hear it."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY
16
College Story / Stress / Frustration
Something broken about college that nobody fixes
A specific frustration from your college experience. Not a general complaint about education in India. Something that happened to you or around you. The more specific, the more people will relate.
What it can look like
"Result was delayed by 6 weeks. No explanation. No update. We just kept checking the portal every day."
"The internal exam syllabus was given 3 days before the exam. The external syllabus is still not fully clear in 4th sem. We just guess and hope."
CAN BE POSTED ANONYMOUSLY

18
Question for Peers / Juniors / Seniors
Second question — directed at students from other colleges
Post 12 was for your own college. This one is aimed outward — at students from other colleges in the hub. Ask something you genuinely want to know about how things work elsewhere. This is what C2 Club is actually for.
What it can look like
"How do internships work at your college? Do they help you find one or is it entirely on you? At our college it's 100% on you."
"What is the one thing your college does better than most? I'll start — ours has genuinely good sports facilities even if nothing else is great."

DAY 10
19
C2 Club Feature / Use Case
One thing you found useful — or one thing you want from C2 Club
Don't promote the app. Just be honest. What did you actually use? What felt useful? Or what is missing that would make it genuinely useful for you? Honest feedback from a real user is more convincing than any promo.
What it can look like
"The hub for our college is actually useful for finding notes and PYQs now. Didn't expect that when I first downloaded it."
"I want C2 Club to have a way to connect directly with seniors from other colleges. That would make it 10x more useful."

AI Meme
Final meme — hit closer to home than post 17
Second and last meme. By now you know your hub audience. Make this one very specific to your course and college, or something universally relatable for any Karnataka college student. No explanation needed if the meme lands.

Use Your Own Idea
The 20 posts above are templates. If you have a better idea that will make your friends engage, post it instead! You know exactly what topics will get your batch talking.
Your job is to bring 20 friends who each post at least one thing in C2 Club. That's it. You don't need to convince them the app is great. You just need to give them one easy reason to post something once.
How to invite
1
Pick the right people

Don't blast everyone. Pick friends who talk a lot — in class, in WhatsApp groups, in college corridors. If they have opinions, they will post. If they are quiet types, skip them for now.

2
Send them a personal message — not a forward

A forward gets ignored. A message that sounds like you wrote it for them gets read. Keep it short. Tell them what you want them to do specifically.

WHAT TO SAY
"Hey, I'm part of this app called C2 Club — it's basically a space where students from different colleges in Karnataka actually talk to each other. I'm building out the [your college] hub there. Download the app and just answer one question — takes 2 minutes. I'll send you the question. Your answer can be anonymous if you want."
3
Share your referral link from inside the app

Go to your profile in C2 Club, find your referral link and send it to them directly after the message. Don't send the link without the message first.

4
Send them one question to answer as their first post

Pick any question from the list below. Copy it, send it to them on WhatsApp. Tell them: put this as the title, write your answer in the body. That is the whole post. Done.

10 Questions — Pick Any One to Send Your Friend
Q1
What is that one unwritten rule in your college that nobody told you but everyone somehow knows?
Easy to answer. Everyone has one. Answers are almost always funny or surprising.
WORKS ANONYMOUSLY TOO
Q2
Describe your typical exam week — from the night before to walking out of the hall.
Universally relatable. Nobody has a boring answer to this one.
Q3
Which professor in your college would survive best if they were a contestant on a reality TV show, and why?
Funny angle. Gets people writing more than they planned. No need to name anyone — just describe the type.
WORKS ANONYMOUSLY TOO
Q4
What is the most useless thing your college made you do that had zero connection to real life?
Everyone has an answer. High relatability. Drives comments from others who had the same experience.
WORKS ANONYMOUSLY TOO
Q5
If your college WhatsApp group was a person, what kind of person would it be?
Creative and fun. Short answers still feel complete. People tag their friends in comments.
Q6
What is the one moment in college where you genuinely felt proud of yourself — big or small?
Slightly more personal but people love sharing small wins. Good balance with the frustration posts.
Q7
Describe the most chaotic day you have had in college so far.
Storytelling format. Answers naturally run long. Fun to read for others.
Q8
What does your college smell like at 8am on a Monday?
Unexpected and specific. Makes people laugh. Very low barrier — anyone can answer this in 3 lines.
Q9
If you could swap one thing about your college with another college you know, what would it be and why?
Cross-college angle. Naturally invites students from other colleges to respond.
Q10
What is something your batch does that would completely confuse an outsider?
Inside joke energy. Specific to their college and batch. High shareability among classmates.
Use Your Own Question
You know your friends and your college best. If you have a question that is guaranteed to make them reply, use that instead of the 10 above!
Other options for their first post
—If there is an active challenge in the Arena with a cash prize, share it — some friends will want to participate on their own.
—If they prefer anonymous, tell them — they can toggle anonymous before posting. No name, no pressure.
—If they have notes or PYQs sitting on their phone, that counts as a post too. Easiest possible entry.
