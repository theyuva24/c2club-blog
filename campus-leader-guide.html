Quick Answers

❓ What if my friends don't want to post?

Totally fine. Your job is to share the challenge — not to convince. If 3 people join and post once, you've already qualified for the base reward.

❓ Is this like those referral programs where I have to spam?

No. You send one message with the challenge link. That's it. No follow-ups. No pressure. If people are curious, they'll join. If not, no worries.

❓ What if I don't have time to post 20 times?

You don't need to. The 20-post template is a menu — pick 3-4 that feel easy. Your hub can thrive with just 5 good posts if the challenge is live.

❓ Can I post anonymously?

Yes. Every post in C2 Club has an "Anonymous" toggle. Use it for honest opinions, frustrations, or sensitive questions.

❓ What if there's no active challenge?

No problem! Just share your college hub with friends using the "10 Questions" below. The goal is connection — the challenge is just a fun accelerator.
