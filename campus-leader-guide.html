Your content · ₹400

Post ideas for your C2 Club hub

10–15 posts is all you need. Use what feels real — skip what doesn't. This is a menu, not a checklist.

💡 Don't Spam

Do not post 10 low-effort items in a single day just to get paid. Payouts are only approved for genuine, spaced-out content that actually adds value to your hub. We verify quality before confirming your task.

Write in your own words. Don't copy the examples. Mix it up! Don't just post 10 of the same thing (like only PYQs). If you post a PYQ today, try a meme tomorrow. A diverse hub is a healthy hub.

Note: Anonymous mode works on every post where it's marked. If you have a better idea that'll get your batch talking — post that instead.

Academics
PYQ
Share a previous year question paper
▾
Find any PYQ on your phone or Drive. Upload it — PDF, photo, screenshot. Write 2 lines: which subject, which sem, one honest line about whether it helped.
What it can look like
"3rd sem Business Law PYQ 2023. Unit 3 repeated exactly. Sharing so someone else doesn't have to hunt."
Notes
Share notes — yours or a friend's
▾
Handwritten, typed, PDF — all fine. If yours are a mess, ask a friend and share theirs. Credit who made them.
What it can look like
"My friend Reena's OB notes. Honestly better than mine. Sharing with permission."
Notes
Formula sheet or cheatsheet — one page
▾
Definitions, formulas, a revision list — something a student could use in the last hour before an exam. If you don't have one, make a simple one. 10 minutes.
What it can look like
"All important formulas for Engineering Maths 2 on one page. Made this the morning of my exam."
PYQ
PYQ with your pattern observations
▾
Upload a PYQ and add what you noticed — which units repeat, what type of questions the examiner seems to like. Even guessing from pattern is more useful than just the file.
What it can look like
"DBMS 3rd sem. Questions from Unit 2 show up every single time. Make of that what you will."
College Life
Opinion
One honest thing about your college — good or bad
▾
Not a review. Just one real observation you'd tell a friend deciding whether to join. End with a question if you want replies. Can be anonymous
What it can look like
"Placements are decent for CS and ECE. For other branches you're mostly on your own. Nobody tells you this during admissions."
Story
Something that happened that others will relate to
▾
A real moment — funny, frustrating, awkward. Tell it like you'd tell a friend over lunch. Specific stories land. Vague ones don't. Can be anonymous
What it can look like
"Submitted an assignment at 11:58pm. Portal crashed at 11:57. Still not over it."
"Group project, 5 members. 2 did everything. 1 we haven't seen since week 3. We all got the same grade."
Relatable
Something everyone silently knows but nobody says
▾
A professor's style, WhatsApp group drama, attendance rules, canteen hours. The more specific to your college, the better. Generic posts get ignored.
What it can look like
"Our class WhatsApp has 87 members. Only 4 actually answer when someone asks something."
"Canteen closes at 4pm. Last class ends at 5pm. Nobody has explained this logic in 3 years."
Hostel
Anything real about living away from home
▾
Hostel rules, PG food, managing money, first time cooking. If you commute — write about that. Everyone will have felt some version of it. Can be anonymous
What it can look like
"First month in PG I ate maggi 14 days straight. Not by choice — just had no idea how to manage money and food."
Event
An event — happened, coming, or wished for
▾
Something that already happened — describe it honestly. Something coming up that others might miss. Or something you wish your college would organise but never does.
What it can look like
"Zero technical events this sem. Not one workshop. Other colleges seem to have something every month."
Questions & Future
Question
Ask something you genuinely want answered
▾
One real question for juniors, seniors, or students from other colleges. Not a quiz. One question. Short. Direct. Can be anonymous
What it can look like
"To final years — did your college actually help with placements or was it all on you?"
"How do internships work at other colleges? At ours it's 100% on you."
Future
What you're planning after college — honest version
▾
Not an inspiring speech. Just what you're actually thinking. MBA, job, exam, startup, no idea — all valid. The confusion is as useful as the clarity. Can be anonymous
What it can look like
"Planning to give CAT this year. Not because I'm sure about MBA — just don't know what else to do."
"Genuinely no idea what comes next. Final year and still figuring it out."
Opinion
Go deeper — something you've thought about more than once
▾
Something that actually bothers you or genuinely impresses you about the system, the culture, the people. Not a rant — an observation. Can be anonymous
What it can look like
"The culture here is to keep quiet and not ask questions. I spent 2 years thinking that was normal. Talked to students from other colleges and realised it's not."
Fun
Meme
Make an AI meme about your college life
▾
Use Canva AI, Adobe Firefly, or a meme template. Make it specific to your course or college — something only your classmates would fully get. Add 1 line caption.

Feedback
One honest thing you'd want from C2 Club
▾
Don't promote the app. Just be honest. What did you actually use? What felt useful? What's missing? Real feedback from real users is the most useful thing you can give.
What it can look like
"The hub is actually useful for finding notes and PYQs. Didn't expect that."
"I want a way to connect directly with seniors from other colleges. That would make this 10x more useful."

"Don't keep on posting the same kind of content. Try to mix it up — if you post a PYQ today, try a meme tomorrow. A healthy mix keeps your college hub alive and interesting."
