Quick Answers

What if my friends don't want to post?
Totally fine. Your job is to share — not to convince. If they see the activity in the hub, they'll likely join in. Even 3 users + 1 post secures your base reward.
Is this a referral program?
No. You're building a community. We use referral links to track who founded which hub. You send one message; that's it. No spamming required.
What if I don't have time to post 20 times?
The 20-post blueprint is a menu. Aim for 5-10 good posts to keep the hub active. Quality matters more than quantity.
Can I post anonymously?
Yes. Every post has an "Anonymous" toggle. We encourage it for honest opinions and stories.
